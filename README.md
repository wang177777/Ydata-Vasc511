# Ydata-Vasc511

Ydata-Vasc511 is a derived research resource created for evaluating multimodal AI model performance in rare and complex cutaneous vascular diseases.

The study dataset was constructed from previously published case reports indexed in PubMed. The public repository is limited to author-generated or de-identified derivative materials that support verification of the associated manuscript, including prompt templates, row-level scoring fields, supplementary methods, web-platform audit logs, and representative de-identified raw-output audit excerpts.

The public materials may include:

- De-identified derived variables and row-level scoring fields.
- Prompt templates and reproducibility notes.
- Supplementary methods, scoring rubrics, statistical summaries, web-platform audit logs, and model-output/scoring verification fields generated by the study team.
